IMPI Digital Cards — One-time Passcode Setup

This runs entirely inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere — it's a plain local file, not a website.

Use a passcode that's easy for you to remember but hard for someone else to guess — a short phrase works well (e.g. four random words). Since your site's code is public, anyone could technically try guessing passcodes against the encrypted result offline, so avoid short PINs or obvious words.
